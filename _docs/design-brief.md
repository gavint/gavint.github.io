# Design Brief — Gavin Terpstra Personal / Services Website

*Prepared for Figma (Make / AI design). Paste the "Quick Prompt" at the bottom directly into Figma Make, or use the full brief to brief a First Draft / manual design.*

## 1. The one-line summary

A clean, modern personal website for **Gavin Terpstra** — a marketing and brand strategist with more than 25 years in the industry who helps businesses grow. It shows what he does, proves it with real results and deep craft, and makes it easy for prospective clients to get in touch.

## 2. Goals (in priority order)

1. **Position him as a growth partner.** A visitor should understand within 10 seconds that Gavin helps companies grow through marketing, branding, and market strategy — not that he's a generalist for hire.
2. **Prove it with results.** Lead with outcomes (grew a brand to an 8-figure valuation, doubled a client base through direct sales, produced published case studies) so credibility is immediate.
3. **Show he understands clients.** Emphasize that he actually talks to customers, understands what they need, and builds the strategy to reach them.
4. **Convert.** Make contacting Gavin effortless — email, book-a-call, and LinkedIn.
5. **Differentiate.** Show he pairs traditional marketing craft with modern AI tools to deliver faster and more affordably than a typical agency.

## 3. Audience

Small-to-midsize business owners and founders who need to grow — get their brand seen, their marketing working, and their sales moving. They value someone who understands their business and can point to real results, not buzzwords.

## 4. Brand personality

Modern, credible, results-driven, and personable. Reads like a sharp growth marketer / fractional CMO with the depth of a career craftsperson — someone who's carried a number and hit it, and who knows design and production from the ink up. Confident but approachable. "A marketer who's actually grown a business — and mastered the craft behind it."

## 5. Positioning note (important)

**Lead with marketing, brand, and growth strategy — and with genuinely understanding clients and what it takes to reach them.** Gavin is also technically capable (Salesforce-native platforms, web, email infrastructure), but that should be a *supporting* credibility point, not a headline. Mention it once as "and he can handle the tech side too, so campaigns actually ship" — do not give IT its own section or equal billing.

## 6. Visual direction

- **Overall feel:** Clean & modern — generous whitespace, sharp typography, one confident accent color, subtle depth.
- **Color palette:**
  - Background: warm off-white (`#FBFAF8`)
  - Ink / text: near-black (`#1A1D21`), soft gray for secondary (`#565C66`)
  - Accent: deep teal-green (`#2F6F5E`), with a light tint (`#E7F0EC`) for chips/icon backgrounds
  - Optional inverted dark panel for the "how I work / AI" band (`#12332B → #1F4A3E`) with a mint highlight (`#8FD3BD`)
- **Typography:** Headings in a modern serif with character — *Fraunces* (or Playfair Display); body & UI in a clean sans — *Inter*.
- **Shapes & style:** Rounded corners (~16px), soft shadows, pill-shaped buttons, small circular/dot accents, subtle hover lift on cards, light scroll-reveal animation. Avoid stock-photo clutter and busy patterns.
- **Imagery:** One professional headshot (hero). Case-study video thumbnails. Simple line icons for the service areas.

## 7. Structure / sections (single-page, top to bottom)

1. **Sticky nav** — wordmark "Gavin Terpstra" left; links (Services, Results, About) + a pill "Get in touch" CTA right.
2. **Hero** — two columns. Left: eyebrow "Marketing · Brand · Growth", a bold serif headline, a one-sentence subhead, two CTAs ("Let's grow your business" + "See the results"). Right: portrait headshot in a rounded card.
3. **Trust / results strip** — a thin bar of proof points: *Grew a brand to an 8-figure valuation · Doubled a client base through direct sales · Salesforce & Marketing Cloud trained · Published case studies.*
4. **What I do (Services)** — three equal cards focused on growth, each with an icon, title, short description, and a bulleted capability list:
   - **Marketing & Growth** — brand growth strategy, email marketing & automation (Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement), lead nurture, campaign strategy & analytics, SEO optimization, websites that draw hundreds of thousands of visitors, personalized print and digital marketing, and market visibility through advertising, networking, and direct outreach.
   - **Brand & Creative** — brand building and design mastered from the production floor up: magazine, poster, flier, and specialty design, marketing collateral, website creation & optimization, event displays and experiential presence, vehicle designs, in-house print management, and case-study production. Formally trained (OTIS College of Art and Design, LA).
   - **Sales & Client Partnership** — direct sales, customer conversations, account management, understanding what clients need and building the plan to reach their market. *(This is the differentiator — Gavin has actually sold the product and managed the accounts.)*
   - *Supporting line under the cards:* "Backed by real technical depth — Salesforce-native platforms, web, and email infrastructure — so the strategy actually ships."
5. **Results / Case Studies** — the strongest section. A 3-up gallery of the case studies Gavin **directed and account-managed**, each an embedded/linked YouTube video with a title, the client/outcome, and a one-line note that Gavin led it:
   - **"How StoreConnect Enables Impact & Scale Natively on Salesforce"** — https://youtu.be/HiX-iyA8_7U
   - **"A B2B that scaled 300% in 3 years"** — https://youtu.be/P4V_eCzRXsQ
   - **"How BCI Wheels scaled B2B sales by automating manual operations"** — https://youtu.be/qaiVbdYYea8
   - Caption for the section: *"Case studies I directed and produced — for accounts I personally managed and helped grow."*
6. **Impact highlights** — a short band of hard numbers/achievements (see section 8) as stat cards or a simple list.
7. **How I work (AI)** — a full-width accent panel: "Traditional marketing craft, accelerated by AI." Three short points: faster turnarounds · human strategy on top · more value per dollar than an agency.
8. **Experience & Craft** — a compact horizontal timeline telling the career journey (see section 8b), with dates: Print & pre-press (1998–2002) → Graphic design (2002→) → Marketing strategy (2005→) → Web marketing & SEO (2008→) → B2B / IT marketing (2017→). Weight the emphasis toward the recent stages; the print stage is one small foundation marker. Keep it visual and skimmable, not a wall of text.
9. **About** — two columns. Left: short first-person bio (see section 9). Right: skill, training & certification chips — highlight **OTIS College of Art and Design**, **Salesforce Trained**, and **Marketing Cloud Account Engagement**, alongside skills like brand strategy, print production, web/SEO, and event design.
10. **Contact** — centered card: headline "Let's grow your business," a friendly line, and three buttons: Email me, Book a call, LinkedIn.
11. **Footer** — copyright, email, LinkedIn.

## 8b. Experience & Craft — career journey (for the timeline section)

A stepped timeline showing progression from foundational craft toward today's high-value work. Each stage is a *start point* that builds on the last (they accumulate — he still does the later ones). Weight the visual emphasis toward the more recent, relevant stages; keep the print/pre-press stage as a single compact foundation column, not two.

1. **1998–2002 — Print & pre-press production.** Foundation on the press floor: offset printing and preparing production-ready files. Where he learned how great work actually gets made. *(Keep this brief — one column, framed as foundational craft.)*
2. **2002 onward — Graphic design.** Magazine, poster, and specialty design for a professional publishing house, later leading designers and translating strategy into executed design.
3. **2005 onward — Marketing strategy.** Planning and directing campaigns; translating marketing goals into execution.
4. **2008 onward — Web marketing & SEO.** Building and optimizing websites (hundreds of thousands of visitors), SEO, and digital campaigns.
5. **2017 onward — B2B marketing in the IT / tech space.** Brand growth, direct sales, account management, and case-study production on a Salesforce-native platform (StoreConnect) — including growing a brand to an 8-figure valuation.

*Design note: give stages 3–5 more visual weight (larger cards / bolder labels); stage 1 is a small foundational marker. Training: OTIS College of Art and Design (Los Angeles); one-to-one mentorship with accomplished LA designers and Publications Design Inc.*
